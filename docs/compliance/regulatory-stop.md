# Regulatory stop (financial data)

Applies when `handles_financial_data: true`.

`/ship` will not open a pull request while this flag is true. Before launch, take professional regulatory advice (for example from a solicitor who works with the Financial Conduct Authority's rules) on whether the product needs authorisation or registration, how customer money and data must be handled, and what you must tell customers.

When you have advice, record who gave it, the date and the conclusion below, then tell Claude to continue. Claude does not decide this.
