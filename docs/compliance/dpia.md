# Data Protection Impact Assessment (template)

Use when `handles_personal_data: true`. Not legal advice. The ICO's DPIA guidance is under review after the Data (Use and Access) Act, so re-read it first (link in `README.md`).

## Do we need one?

A DPIA is needed when processing is likely to result in a high risk to people. The ICO treats innovative technology, which can include AI, as a factor. It requires a DPIA when that is combined with other high-risk criteria, such as large scale, vulnerable people, or invisible processing.

**Record the decision either way.** If you decide not to do a DPIA, write down why.

## If needed

1. **Describe the processing**: what, why, how, volume, who is affected. Link `data-map.md`.
2. **Consultation**: who was asked (users, security, a data protection adviser).
3. **Necessity and proportionality**: lawful basis, whether less data would do, how people are informed.
4. **Risks to people**: likelihood and severity for each.
5. **Measures to reduce each risk** and the risk that remains.
6. **Sign-off**: who approved, date, review date.
7. **If a high risk remains** after measures: take advice on whether to consult the ICO before launch.
