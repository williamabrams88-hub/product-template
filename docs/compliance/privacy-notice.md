# Privacy notice (template)

Use when `handles_personal_data: true`. Write it in plain language for the people it concerns. Have it checked before launch. Not legal advice.

1. **Who we are** and how to contact us about your data.
2. **What we collect** and where it comes from. Match `data-map.md`.
3. **Why we use it** and the lawful basis for each purpose.
4. **Who we share it with**, including providers who process it for us.
5. **Transfers outside the UK**, and the safeguard used.
6. **How long we keep it**.
7. **Your rights**: to be informed, access, correct, delete, restrict, object, move your data, and rights about automated decisions.
8. **How to complain**: to us first, and to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
9. **Changes to this notice** and the date of the latest version.
